Demographically weighted distribution of IPA symbols

The dataset covers every PHOIBLE source-attested language. L counts source-attested languages, deduplicated by ISO 639-3 (by Glottocode for uncoded languages). P is demographically weighted and is computed only over the demographic-certain subset — languages that can be linked to a verified population estimate. Languages without a certain estimate are still shown and counted in L, but carry no population and never enter P; they are not counted as zero. P includes only green verified/curated mappings and amber alternative realizations; blue unresolved source attestations are excluded. For click symbols, L aggregates complete PHOIBLE click segments by their anterior click component, while P still follows the green-and-amber rule. When a language is selected, the chart distinguishes verified or curated phoneme–grapheme mappings, alternative realizations, source-attested unresolved symbols, and unattested sounds.

Source-aware, language-by-language verification model. A language phoneme may be written with several graphemes, and a grapheme may represent several phonemes. Likewise, one IPA phone may realize several phonemes and one phoneme may have several IPA realizations. Green is reserved for an explicit or curated phoneme–grapheme mapping; amber marks an explicit alternative realization; blue means that the exact symbol is present in at least one PHOIBLE inventory but its cross-source canonical interpretation has not been independently resolved; red means not attested in the current aggregated inventories/model. In language mode, green indicates a canonical phoneme, amber an alternative or allophonic transcription mapped to a canonical phoneme, and red a sound that is not present. The linkage starts from the ISO 639-3 codes available in PHOIBLE; Glottocodes remain associated with inventories and are shown in the details so that language identity can be verified. Two-letter CLDR codes, macrolanguages, and selected bibliographic codes are normalized to the ISO 639-3 code used by PHOIBLE through an explicit, reviewed mapping table. Only languages with both a PHOIBLE inventory and a positive demographic estimate are included. Click symbols are the exception to exact-symbol matching: PHOIBLE represents a click as a complete segment combining posterior and anterior articulations, so each click cell aggregates every PHOIBLE segment containing that anterior click component. L is deduplicated by ISO 639-3. P is an index weighted by estimated speakers and includes only green verified/curated mappings and amber alternative realizations. Blue source-attested unresolved records are excluded from P. The demographic source may include L1 and L2 speakers, and multilingual people may therefore contribute to more than one language. It is not a count of unique individuals and must not be described as an “L1 base.” IPA variants with diacritics, length, aspiration, or palatalization remain separate.
